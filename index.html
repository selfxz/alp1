Personal Style

Part 1 · Fashion (Unit 4)

Ideal Neighborhood

Part 2 · Where I Want to Live (Unit 5)

  • Walkable: Stores close by so I can run errands or go grocery shopping on foot.
  • Safe & Accessible: Wide sidewalks & safe bike lanes for pedestrians and cyclists — no terrible traffic.
  • My Dream: A quiet, green neighborhood. For me, that would be magical.

My Future Goals

Part 3 · Future Timeline (Unit 6)
